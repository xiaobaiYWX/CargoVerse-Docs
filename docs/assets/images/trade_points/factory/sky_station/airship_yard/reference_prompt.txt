Asset type: one four-panel Minecraft architectural reference board for 飞艇总装与航修港 (Airship Assembly and Repair Port).
Primary request: Design four distinct, genuinely buildable vanilla Minecraft 1.21.1 industrial architectural shells. They belong to one high-tier aviation trade point, but are separate structures. Emphasize structural hierarchy, deep window reveals, exposed stepped trusses, practical walks and convincing support, not machinery decoration. Minecraft voxel construction with ordinary cubes, slabs, stairs, glass and iron bars; crisp vanilla textures, neutral daylight, neutral studio background, orthographic three-quarter views from front and right. Show the entire footprint of each building. One coherent 2x2 sheet with panel numbers "01", "02", "03", "04", no other text.
Palette: pale stone and white/light-gray terracotta walls, polished andesite supports, dark polished-deepslate framing, muted cyan terracotta for aviation identity. Blue concrete only as tiny signs or short stripes, no bright concrete walls. Small ordinary lanterns. Never use resource blocks (iron/gold/copper/diamond/emerald), ores, beacons, sea lanterns or high-value functional blocks. Cheap architecture only.
01 upper left: AIRSHIP ASSEMBLY DOCK. A long OPEN-TOP U-shaped shipyard, approximately 33 blocks wide by 39 long. Two rows of tall exposed longitudinal trusses flank a long empty central assembly lane. Narrow side service decks and elevated side catwalks; a reachable stair connects to one catwalk. Side structure heights step up toward the rear. A closed transverse workshop at the rear joins the two wings, with a visible public reception entrance on the front end of the left wing, so visitors need not cross the assembly lane. The front has NO overhead gate beam and the middle has NO roof. A low empty keel cradle made of beams, not a complete ship, may occupy a small part of the middle. Strong aviation dock silhouette, not a conventional closed hangar. Keep all high walks supported and protected.
02 upper right: PROPULSION SERVICE SHED, approximately 25 by 25. A single-slope open maintenance shed, roof low at the front and distinctly higher at the back. Exposed three-bay side frames. Empty vertical component stands at one side, empty low support plinth at the other, five-block-wide clear center aisle, broad open front and a side service opening. The asymmetric roof and rear frame give a strong recognizable silhouette. NO engine, propeller, vehicle or other equipment; only empty structural stands.
03 lower left: AVIONICS INSPECTION BUILDING, approximately 25 by 23. Compact two-storey instrument building: separated receiving and instrument rooms below, upper observation gallery with a long continuous window band facing the dock, a slight cantilever supported by beams, shallow flat roof with recessed edge. A three-block-wide exterior stair along the right rear side reaches the upper level with a clearly usable landing and upper doorway. The stairs are away from the public front entrance. Pale walls, dark thin frames and muted cyan terracotta service strips. No antennas required, no furniture or terminals. The upper gallery is an accessible real storey.
04 lower right: GROUND-SERVICE COURT, approximately 29 by 25. An L-shaped open yard with a modest ground-crew building along the left edge, a shallow rear open canopy for future ground vehicles, and a broad empty maneuvering court in front/right. Low railings distinguish display/maintenance space from through pedestrian routes. One short projecting side service balcony or dock finger supported from below. Mostly open sky, no tall enclosing hall. Empty vehicle bays only; no vehicles, crates or cargo terminal. The left crew building and rear canopy have distinctly different roof heights.
All main floors are four blocks above bottom supports. Minimal fixtures; focus on layout, structural frame, walls, roofs and reachable stairs. No populated interiors, armor stands, characters, actual aircraft, terrain or surrounding buildings. No H marks or aircraft cargo aprons in these four panels: the station reuses one EXISTING separate shared H-marked cargo platform. Do not invent an additional receiving/shipping platform. Do not repeat the same box shape four times. Everything stays orthogonal to a block grid; diagonal trusses are stepped blocks, never thin realistic metal rods.
