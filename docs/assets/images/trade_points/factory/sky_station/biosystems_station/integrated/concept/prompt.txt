Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: single master Minecraft architectural reference board for a buildable Java 1.21.1 structure.
Primary request: Design the complete Oasis Bioengineering Station operated by Verdant BioSystems (VBS), a modest suspended ecological and medical facility. A cohesive single station on a thin engineered floating deck with visible underside beams and corner brackets, NOT a giant island and NOT many unrelated buildings.
Composition: one large clear front-left three-quarter orthographic view occupying 80% of a landscape board, plus one small roof plan of the EXACT SAME layout in the upper-right. Plain warm grey background, neutral daylight, crisp visible unit blocks, no fog, no depth blur. Both views same entrances, roof bays and platform. Title only "VBS / OASIS BIOSYSTEMS".
Design in only three related visual groups: (1) left half a pair of joined elongated greenhouse naves, clear pitched glass roofs with refined thin white stone ribs and dark timber low plinths, repeated structural bays, a small projecting glass entrance vestibule at the front; (2) a lower L-shaped clean medical and sample wing attached along the back and right side of the glass halls, pale warm masonry walls, narrow long windows, layered flat grey roofs and one short roof maintenance stair; (3) a single exposed shared cargo H landing pad on the right-front deck, connected to the vestibule and wing by a clear walking path. One roof nave slightly taller than its neighbour. Greenhouse is the architectural focal point, lab wing low and restrained. No tall tower.
Scale budget around 70 blocks wide, 24 blocks high including suspended foundation, 55 blocks deep; 76 is a soft long-side reference, never a target to fill. Ground deck elevation 4. Greenhouse clear floor is one level, eaves about 7 blocks above floor, ridge another 6 blocks above eaves. Each nave about 13-15 blocks across, five longitudinal structural spans of about 6 blocks. Show ordinary two-block-high door leaves (exclude transom), one-block-thick posts, three-block-wide stair, visible 1-block construction rhythm, not tiny doors on an enormous monument. The front vestibule must visibly retain ordinary double wooden/glazed door proportions. The cargo pad is a single 25x25 block flat area with flush white H and border, clear sky above, equipment reservation spaces outside its perimeter. Avoid annotation-dimension labels that claim exact measurement; a short unit scale bar near front doors and block grid in the small plan are enough.
Materials by function: transparent glazing, smooth quartz and calcite ribs and medical walls, polished andesite sills and grey slab roof edges, stripped dark timber lower panels, stone brick deck and iron-bar safety rail. VBS yellow-green/lime and white are ONLY small entrance-sign and short identification-strip accents; do not tint all glazing green or paint whole walls lime. Do not use iron/gold/diamond/netherite/emerald storage blocks or beacons. Actual iron bars allowed.
Scope: finished exterior architectural shell, supporting frame, windows, real access stairs and landings, roof gutters/ridge caps, lights, deck protection. Through glass show useful empty floor and a few empty low grow-bed outlines, not crops or elaborate equipment. No interior furniture, people, creatures, vehicles, containers, huge pipes or extra towers. Preserve circulation and pad airspace. Strong handsome conservatory silhouette with disciplined human-scale details, no photographic real-world shapes that cannot be built from Minecraft blocks.
