Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Minecraft Java 1.21.1 structure reference board for one integrated trade station
Primary request: Design one coherent suspended Horizon Research Institute field expedition station, intended to be rebuilt block by block in vanilla Minecraft. The whole station is an irregular platform approximately 70 blocks wide, 62 blocks deep and 27 blocks high; these are a budget, not decorative text. Simplify the station into three readable visual groups: a front shared aviation receiving platform, a connected registration-and-sample-research spine across the rear, and a side field-instrument group consisting of one compact drone hangar plus one raised observation deck with a static parabolic dish.
Scene/backdrop: clean neutral studio background, isolated floating station with no terrain, vehicles, people, vegetation or surrounding buildings
Subject: front-left 25x25 shared H landing/cargo pad with open airspace and two equipment strips behind its boundary; rear-central low research spine with a public registration entrance, one long clerestory sample hall and two or three clearly repeated structural bays; right-side compact barrel-roof drone hangar with one wide opening and empty interior; left-rear raised observation deck reached by a real three-block-wide staircase, supporting a block-buildable parabolic dish and a small weather room; covered connectors join all occupied spaces without crossing the H pad
Style/medium: polished Minecraft vanilla block architectural concept, orthographic-like voxel render, believable block scale, functional expedition architecture rather than science-fiction megastructure
Composition/framing: one single reference board. A large front three-quarter axonometric view occupies about 75 percent of the board and is the authoritative shape. One smaller roofless top-down plan inset shows exactly the same footprint, entrances, stairs, platform and room divisions. One small scale-detail inset shows a complete two-block-high door, a three-block-wide stair, one six-block structural bay and a sealed slab eave junction. All views must depict the same design and orientation.
Lighting/mood: bright overcast daylight, clear edges and readable interior openings, minimal shadows
Color palette: functional off-white and pale stone walls, gray stone base, dark gray roof and frame; Horizon Research Institute blue and medium gray repeated as visible accent strips at entrances, window bands, hangar portal and dish support, never as the main wall or entire roof color
Materials/textures: vanilla-like smooth sandstone or calcite wall panels, stone brick and polished andesite base/frame, oxidized or weathered copper and dark deepslate roof portions, continuous light-gray or blue-tinted glass without iron-bar grids, iron bars only as independent guardrails
Constraints: visible two-block-high doors and countable stairs; structural columns continue uninterrupted from base to beam; hanging lights sit at bay centers below independent ceiling anchors and never replace columns or walls; every slab roof edge has a full-block closure course with no half-block gaps; each repeated window group and portal is locally symmetric about its own bay axis even though the whole station is asymmetrical; real landings at both ends of every stair; empty interiors with no furniture or machines; all structure stays outside the 25x25 H airspace; no resource storage blocks or beacon
Avoid: multiple alternative designs, oversized campus, four separate detached houses, excessive micro-detail, illegible dimensions, fantasy curves impossible in blocks, glass mixed with iron-bar mullions, unsupported roofs, clipped columns, half-slab holes, accidental one-block asymmetry, cargo clutter, text labels, watermark
